# Feedback request for real applicants

Send this only to people who have really applied or worked through Role Glide. Quote someone only with their written yes.

## Message

Hi [first name], thanks for applying to Role Glide.

Could you spare two minutes to tell us how it went? Good, bad or in between, we want the honest version.

1. How did you hear back from us, and how long did it take?
2. What was helpful? What wasn't?
3. If you worked a role, roughly what hours and pay did you actually get? (Skip this if you'd rather not say.)

May we quote you on our website? If yes, please reply with:
- the first name (or initials) you're happy for us to show
- your province
- "Yes, you can quote me"

We won't publish anything without your yes.

## Adding a review to the site

Open `app.js`, find `const REVIEWS = [];` and add one entry per approved review:

    { name: 'First name L.', place: 'Province', stars: 4, text: 'Their exact words.' }

Keep the wording as the person wrote it. Keep critical reviews too.

## Chat screenshots

Only with the person's permission, with names and phone numbers blurred. Do not mock them up.
