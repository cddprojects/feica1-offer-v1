07 — QUICK ANSWERS

About remote work.
About applying.

A few answers before you fill in the form.

What does WorkNest Canada do?+

We are the bridge between Canadian companies and talent. Companies tell us who they need for online operations, such as a food and beverage business that needs help with online orders and customer messages. We connect them with people whose skills and hours fit. You keep your day job.

Who employs me?+

The Canadian company, not WorkNest. Pay, hours and duties are set by that company and shown to you in writing before you decide.

Do you charge me anything?+

We never ask you for a deposit, a transfer or a payment. If anyone asking for money says they are us, it is not us.

Can I keep my current job?+

Yes, that is the idea. A second income works best beside a job, not instead of one. Check each opportunity’s hours and conditions, and any obligations to your current employer.

Why would I want a second job, really?+

Rent and groceries are the answer most people give. The better question is what you want that one paycheque will not reach: a down payment, a debt-free date, a cottage weekend, a trip home, a business of your own. Statistics Canada found that about 1 in 7 multiple jobholders in 2023 worked a second job for work they were passionate about.

Is it normal to have more than one income?+

It is common. Statistics Canada reports about 1.1 million employed Canadians (5.6%) held more than one job in 2023. About a third said financial necessity was the main reason, and about a third said extra income.

Are there government bonuses for remote or online work?+

No. Canada has no bonus or reward for working remotely. Any bonus would come from an individual employer and is not guaranteed. What does exist is practical: federally regulated employees can ask in writing for flexible hours or location after six months, and employers must answer within 30 days. That right to ask does not cover most private-sector jobs.

Do I report the extra income?+

Yes. Self-employment and side income is reported on your tax return, including tips. Reporting it accurately also keeps your benefits and credits correct. Check the Canada Revenue Agency’s guidance for your situation. This site does not give tax advice.

How much can I earn?+

As much as your hours and the role’s rate allow. It is simple math: rate × paid hours × weeks. Use the numbers section. The starting example is CAD $25 an hour for 10 hours a week over four weeks, which is CAD $1,000 before taxes. At 15 hours it is CAD $1,500. These are illustrations, not promises. Actual pay rates, paid hours and availability are confirmed before you decide.

Where in Canada is this available?+

Every province and territory. Select yours in the application form.

How fast will I hear back?+

You get a confirmation on screen right after you submit. A real person then writes to you, even if the answer is no.

Do I need a phone call to apply?+

No. Complete the online application form with your details and preferred hours. This preview form does not send your application yet.
