01 — WHY YOU NEED A SECOND JOB

Six reasons
one paycheque isn’t enough.

Over a million employed Canadians already work more than one job (Statistics Canada, 2023). About 1 in 3 do it for extra income. About 1 in 7 do it for work they love. Here is what they know.

↗01

Because one employer shouldn’t hold your whole life.

One meeting invite and your only income is gone. A second one means you never start from zero.

A SAFETY NET
✳02

Because prices don’t wait for your review.

Canadian prices rose 2.1% in 2025 (Statistics Canada). A raise smaller than that is a pay cut. A second income is the one raise you set yourself.

A RAISE YOU CONTROL
☀03

Because your goal has a price and a date.

At CAD $250 a week, CAD $10,000 takes about 40 weeks. Without it, “someday” has no date at all. Try your own numbers below.

YOUR DATE
☾04

Because it’s a free trial of the life you want.

Test a new field, learn a new skill, build a plan B, all before you risk the job you have.

A TRIAL RUN
⌁05

Because walk-away money changes how you work.

When your day job isn’t your only lifeline, you can say no to the bad shift, the bad offer and the bad week.

YOUR LEVERAGE
◎06

Because the commute is gone.

Ten hours a week is 520 hours a year. At home, every one of them is a working hour, not a drive.
