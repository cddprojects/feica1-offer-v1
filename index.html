04 — QUICK ANSWERS

Remote work, side jobs
and getting paid.

A few answers before you fill in the form.

How do I earn money from home with WorkNest?+

You earn money online by working part-time for Canadian companies from home. We are the bridge between those companies and talent. Companies tell us who they need for online operations, such as a food and beverage business that needs help with online orders and customer messages. We connect them with people whose skills and hours fit. You keep your day job.

Is this freelance or remote work, and who employs me?+

It is remote, part-time, work-from-home work. You choose your role and hours, and the Canadian company, not WorkNest, sets the terms for each role. Pay, hours and duties are set by that company and shown to you before you decide.

What does it cost me?+

Applying costs you nothing.

Can I do a side job and keep my current job?+

Yes, that is the idea. A second income works best beside a job, not instead of one. Check each opportunity’s hours and conditions, and any obligations to your current employer.

Why would I want a second job, really?+

Rent and groceries are the answer most people give. The better question is what kind of life you want it to buy: the trip home, the easier later years, the weekends that are properly yours. Statistics Canada found that about 1 in 7 multiple jobholders in 2023 worked a second job for work they were passionate about.

Is it normal to have more than one income?+

It is common. Statistics Canada reports about 1.1 million employed Canadians (5.6%) held more than one job in 2023. About a third said financial necessity was the main reason, and about a third said extra income.

Are there government bonuses for remote or online work?+

No. Canada has no bonus or reward for working remotely. Any bonus would come from an individual employer and is not guaranteed. What does exist is practical: federally regulated employees can ask in writing for flexible hours or location after six months, and employers must answer within 30 days. That right to ask does not cover most private-sector jobs.

Do I report the extra income?+

Yes. Self-employment and side income is reported on your tax return, including tips. Reporting it accurately also keeps your benefits and credits correct. Check the Canada Revenue Agency’s guidance for your situation. This site does not give tax advice.

How much money can I make online?+

As much as your hours and the role’s rate allow. It is simple math: rate × paid hours × weeks. Use the earnings section to see example monthly totals for different kinds of work and hours. These are illustrations, not promises. The company sets the real rate and hours for each opening, and you see them before you decide.

Where in Canada is this available?+

Every province and territory. Select yours in the application form.

What happens after I submit?+

You get a confirmation on screen right after you submit. If your skills and hours fit a role, we contact you.

I applied and haven’t heard back. Why?+

Roles open from time to time, and we reach out when one fits your skills and hours. If you haven’t heard from us, there isn’t a match for you yet. You stay on file, so you’re considered when the next one opens.

Do I need a phone call to apply?+

No. Complete the online application form with your details and preferred hours.
