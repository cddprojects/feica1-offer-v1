SCAM-PROOF PROMISE

Five things
we will never do.

Job fraud cost Canadians $50.6 million in 2025, according to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre. These are its warning signs. We never do any of them.

  1. 01

    Ask for a deposit.

    No “upgrade fee” and no “unlock payment”. Ever.

  2. 02

    Ask for crypto.

    You never send cryptocurrency to get work.

  3. 03

    Make you install software.

    No “task” apps. No fake balance you cannot withdraw.

  4. 04

    Ask you to recruit others.

    Your result never depends on bringing in friends.

  5. 05

    Route money through you.

    No receiving or forwarding money through your bank account.
